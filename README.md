# Time Series Analysis on Unemployment Rate in Italy

This project focuses on the comprehensive time series analysis and forecasting of the monthly unemployment rate in Italy. The goal is to identify the most suitable model for analyzing and predicting this crucial economic indicator by examining underlying trends, seasonal effects, and the influence of complementary economic factors.

The study evaluates a range of models, including:

Linear Regression 

Holt-Winters Exponential Smoothing 

ARIMA 

Non-Parametric Regression (LOESS, Smoothing Splines, and Generalized Additive Models)

Gradient Boosting 
 

# Dataset: 

The analysis utilizes the monthly total unemployment rate in Italy as the target variable, complemented by several economic indicators to provide a more holistic understanding of influencing dynamics. These indicators include:

-Retail Trade Volume

-Harmonized Index of Consumer Prices (HICP)

-Production in Industry

-EUR-USD Exchange Rate

# Performance Evaluation:

Models are quantitatively compared using standard metrics on training and testing datasets, highlighting the strengths and limitations of each method.
